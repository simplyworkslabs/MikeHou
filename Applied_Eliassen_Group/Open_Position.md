Description:
Remote

Our client seeks a Senior Android Developer to lead the design, development, and maintenance of core Android applications. The role will collaborate with product managers, UX/UI designers, and backend engineers to deliver scalable, high-performance, and user-friendly experiences. The developer will mentor junior and mid-level engineers and guide technical architecture decisions.

We can facilitate w2 and corp-to-corp consultants. For our w2 consultants, we offer a great benefits package that includes Medical, Dental, and Vision benefits, 401k with company matching, and life insurance.

Rate: $60.00 to $70.00/hr. w2

Responsibilities:
Design & Build: Develop advanced, resilient, and responsive Android applications from scratch or enhance existing large-scale codebases.
Architecture: Own application architecture such as MVVM, MVP, or Clean Architecture and ensure adherence to SOLID principles and design patterns.
API Integration: Integrate with RESTful APIs, JSON, WebSockets, and backend services.
Optimization & Quality: Profile performance, address memory leaks, reduce crashes and ANRs, and implement unit and instrumentation tests using JUnit and Espresso.
Collaboration & Mentorship: Participate in Agile ceremonies, perform code reviews, and mentor team members.
CI/CD & Deployment: Manage Git-based source control, contribute to automated CI/CD pipelines, and support Google Play Store release management.

Experience Requirements:
5+ to 8+ years of professional mobile development experience focused on Android.
Proficiency in Kotlin and Java.
Extensive experience with Android SDK, Android Studio, Gradle, and modern UI frameworks including Jetpack Compose and Material Design.
Experience with Retrofit, Room, SQLite, and asynchronous data handling.
Preferred: Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud Platform.
Preferred: Familiarity with financial or enterprise-scale applications.
Preferred: Published apps on the Google Play Store.

Education Requirements:
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or equivalent practical experience.
